# Abundio product tour — voice-over script

Timed to the presentation in `index.html`. Each line is written to land
comfortably inside its scene's on-screen duration (`data-dur`) at a relaxed
narration pace of ~150 words/minute, leaving a beat of silence before the next
slide.

**Voice direction:** confident and warm, developer-to-developer. Not a hard
sell — like a colleague showing you a tool they genuinely like. Let the
em-dashes breathe; land the gradient phrases ("abundance of productivity",
"one glance away") a touch slower.

Total runtime ≈ 1:47.

---

## Introduction

**Slide 1 · Title** — *7.5s*
> Meet Abundio — an abundance of productivity. A home base for
> project-centric, AI-assisted development.

---

## Features

**Slide 2 · One window** — *12s*
> Everything you need lives in one window. Shells, coding agents, your editor,
> and your git workflow — side by side, all scoped to the project you're in.

**Slide 3 · AI agents** — *13s*
> Coding agents are first-class here. Abundio auto-detects Claude Code,
> Copilot, Gemini, Aider, Codex, and OpenCode — each running beside your shell
> with a live status dot, so you always know who's working, waiting, or done.

**Slide 4 · The fleet** — *11s*
> Running dozens of projects? The overview bar keeps your whole fleet one
> glance away — live counts of every workspace, agent, terminal, and pull
> request.

**Slide 5 · Splits** — *11s*
> Split panes horizontally or vertically, nest them as deep as you like, and
> organize across tabs. Every layout persists — so you never lose your place.

**Slide 6 · Editor & explorer** — *10s*
> A Monaco-powered editor and tree explorer are built right in. Click a file
> path in any terminal, and it opens instantly.

**Slide 7 · Git & GitHub** — *11.5s*
> Your git workflow lives here too. Review changed files, switch branches, read
> inline diffs, and check the pull requests waiting on you — without ever
> leaving the app.

**Slide 8 · Navigation** — *10.5s*
> And everything's a keystroke away. Command-K jumps to any command, workspace,
> or agent. Command-P opens any file. Command-Shift-F searches the whole
> project.

**Slide 9 · Markdown & notes** — *11s*
> Write docs and watch them render live, Mermaid diagrams and all. And every
> workspace gets its own notes — so your context and ideas always stay close.

---

## Outro

**Slide 10 · Close** — *9s*
> Abundio. Run an abundance of work. Free and open source — on macOS, Windows,
> and Linux.

---

### Notes for recording

- The scene engine auto-advances on `data-dur`; record the VO first, then nudge
  any scene's `data-dur` to match your actual take if a line runs long.
- Spoken keystrokes ("Command-K") eat more time than they read — slide 8 is the
  tightest; don't rush the rest to compensate.
- Leave ~0.5s of silence at the head of slide 1 and the tail of slide 10 so the
  logo reveal and the closing hold aren't stepped on.
